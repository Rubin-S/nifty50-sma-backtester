# Point-in-time vs today's-list universe: Nifty 50, 2018-01-01 to 2026-05-15

Generated by `python scripts/run_backtest.py --compare` (and `--missing-policy drop|proxy`).
Raw numbers: `universe_comparison*.json`.

**Setup.** Each Nifty 50 stock gets weight 1/N_t (N_t = members on day t) while its 20-day SMA is above its
50-day SMA, otherwise its slot is cash. Signal at close t, trade at t+1, 5 bps on turnover, rf = 0,
adjusted closes from Yahoo Finance. Period ends 2026-05-15 because that is where the membership dataset's
coverage ends. "Today's list" = NSE's `ind_nifty50list.csv` downloaded 2026-10-23, applied to every date.

**Index churn in the period:** 21 additions and 21 deletions (18 of today's 50 were not members on 2018-01-01).

## Caveats

* **Missing prices understate the bias.** Only one past member has no Yahoo history: **HDFC** (HDFC Ltd,
  member until the HDFC Bank merger on 2023-07-13; 1,512 member-days in the period). Under the default
  `cash` policy its slot sits in cash. `drop` and `proxy` (HDFCBANK prices as a stand-in) move PIT CAGR by
  only about 0.1 pp. HDFC was a merger, not a collapse, so it is not the kind of name that drives survivorship bias.
* **Renamed/merged names** are handled by the dataset's canonical symbols, which match Yahoo's current
  tickers: LTM (= LTIMindtree / LTI history), INDUSTOWER (= Bharti Infratel pre-2020), TMPV (= Tata Motors),
  SAMMAANCAP (= Indiabulls Housing), ETERNAL (= Zomato), SHRIRAMFIN. None of them were dropped.
* **Unadjusted corporate action:** TMPV fell 40.2% on 2025-10-14 because of the Tata Motors CV demerger, not a
  market move. That day's return is set to 0 in both modes. Other big one-day moves seen in Yahoo data that
  were left alone: TRENT -33% on 2026-01-01 (not checked; it is in both universes) and VEDL -65% on 2026-04-30
  (not a member then).
* **BSE is in today's list** (added at the Sep 2026 review, after the dataset's coverage ends) and returned
  about 47x over the period. Using the dataset's own 2026-05-15 list instead (WIPRO instead of BSE) still gives
  SMA CAGR 11.42% / Sharpe 1.25 and buy-and-hold CAGR 19.79%. So most of the gap is not one stock.
* Daily rebalancing to 1/N with costs on |change in weight| only (price drift between days is ignored).
  Newly listed names (JIOFIN, ETERNAL, ...) trade only once they have 50 days of history.
* Membership before 2018 comes from a lower-confidence "snapshot_floor" in the dataset. That does not matter
  here because the backtest starts in 2018.
